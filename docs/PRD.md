# Entrepreneurial Collaboration — PRD summary

A mobile-first, global, English-first platform where entrepreneurs and collaborators can discover each other and form teams around ideas. Any person may lead one idea and join another. Discovery supports both **find people for my idea** and **find an idea/team to join**. Requests to join are decided by the owner/team. AI provides optional assistance, never autonomous decisions. Initial beta target: approximately 30–50 balanced participants. North-star metric: new collaborations reaching a mutually agreed meaningful milestone each month.

## MVP
Authentication, profiles, idea creation/publication, visibility-aware discovery, join requests and decisions, membership, basic messaging, milestones, essential safety/privacy and analytics. Prioritize usability, access control and trust.

## Exclusions
Autonomous team decisions, equity/legal contracts, investment marketplace, payments, video calling, comprehensive project management, opaque compatibility scores and follower leaderboards.
